    Behind direction 02

    Expression in layers

    AI-created exploration for the supplied VOID brief. Intended effects are hypotheses, not customer findings.

    Reference

    April Greiman, Does it make sense? / Design Quarterly #133, 1986 ↗

    The inspected reverse montage juxtaposes pixel textures, overlapping photographic rectangles and text blocks at sharply different scales.

    Design choices

    An oversized background name crosses a framed product image. Serif italics interrupt bold sans-serif type, while offset paper panels and a dotted texture quote print collage. Controls remain upright in their own clear area.

    Intended audience effect

    Suggest that the blank shirt can belong to an individual artistic language.

    Risk

    Layering can compete with the product; on narrow screens the collage simplifies to preserve reading and control access.
