    Behind direction 01

    Ordered expression

    AI-created exploration for the supplied VOID brief. Intended effects are hypotheses, not customer findings.

    Reference

    Josef Müller-Brockmann, Musica Viva, 1958 ↗

    The inspected poster uses a large diamond field, small repeated colored squares and aligned sans-serif information.

    Design choices

    A repeatable two-column grid separates the product from its controls. A geometric diamond anchors the shirt. One sans-serif family and ruled labels make the reading order explicit.

    Intended audience effect

    Give creative visitors a clear, gallery-like view of the blank canvas.

    Risk

    Its controlled rhythm may feel too institutional for visitors seeking experimentation.
