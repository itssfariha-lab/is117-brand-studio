VOID transforms an ordinary white T-shirt into a canvas for personal expression. This board uses the storefront’s actual CSS tokens, type, controls and SVG. Postmodernist direction selected by the learner; brief and logo approved by the learner.

Direction & voice

For creative people who use alternative streetwear for self-expression. Layered framing and contrasting typography express an artistic, unconventional point of view.

Archetype: Creator. The brand’s recurring character invites making and expression; it does not classify every customer. Voice: bold, artistic, experimental.

Voice in use: “Your body. Your canvas.” Expressive headlines sit beside plain instructions such as “Choose a size.”

An opening in the ordinary.

AI-drawn, learner-approved SVG: geometric letters with an interrupted O. The opening suggests space for an individual mark. No trademark clearance is claimed.

Same transparent, single-fill asset in every presentation. Preserve the open O and leave at least one letter-stroke of clear space.
